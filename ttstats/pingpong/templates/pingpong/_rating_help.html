{% icon "info" size="sm" css="text-muted-foreground" %} Provisional and unrated, explained

Provisional means the player has fewer than {{ provisional_matches }} rated matches in that format, ever. Until then each result moves their rating further than usual, so it finds their level quickly. The marker disappears at {{ provisional_matches }}; nothing else about the rating changes when it does.

Unrated means no rated match in that format yet (this season, on a season table). It is not the starting rating of a new player -- there is simply no number yet.

Singles and doubles are rated separately: winning with a strong partner says little about your singles game. That is why someone settled in one format can be provisional, or unrated, in the other. A new season starts from your all-time rating in that format, pulled part of the way back toward the middle, and does not make you provisional again.
